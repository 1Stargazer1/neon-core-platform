NEON//CORE / PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

How the current NEON//CORE website handles information.

01 / ACCESS

Accounts and payments

There are currently no user accounts or registration, and no active payments or subscriptions. The pricing page describes proposed plans only.

02 / ON YOUR DEVICE

Browser preferences

NEON//CORE uses your browser's LocalStorage to save preferences such as your theme, favorites, recently used tools, and selected category. You can clear this information through your browser's site data settings.

03 / QR CODES

Local generation

QR codes are generated locally in your browser. The contents you enter are not sent to a NEON//CORE server. Anyone who scans a code can read its contents, so take care when sharing sensitive information.

04 / EXTERNAL RESOURCES

Third-party services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, and the QR code generator loads its QR-generation library from jsDelivr. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and details of the request. Their own privacy practices apply.

No analytics or advertising trackers were identified in the existing project audit.

05 / QUESTIONS

Contact

For privacy questions, email NeonCore094@gmail.com. When you email us, you share your email address and any information included in your message.
