HOW TO INSPECT MANUAL TEST

Look for pixels that differ.

View each solid colour and look for dots that stay a different colour from the surrounding area. Gradients and checkerboards can help reveal banding or display artifacts. Use Previous and Next, or focus the test surface and press the left or right arrow key. In fullscreen, controls hide after about three seconds; move the mouse, touch the screen, or press a key to reveal them. Tests remain still until you choose another one.

This tool cannot automatically detect physical pixel defects; inspection is visual only. Browser fullscreen does not guarantee that the operating system displays every physical pixel. Do not press or tap on the screen. Use a comfortable brightness and stop if the display is uncomfortable to view.
