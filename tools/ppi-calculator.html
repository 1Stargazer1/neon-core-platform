THE CALCULATION

PPI measures how densely pixels are packed. It's calculated from the display's resolution and diagonal size using the Pythagorean theorem.

PPI = √(width² + height²) ÷ diagonal (inches)

Pixel pitch is the physical distance between adjacent pixels.

Pixel pitch (mm) = 25.4 ÷ PPI

This calculation assumes square pixels and the stated diagonal size. Actual perceived sharpness depends on viewing distance and individual eyesight.
