DIAGNOSTIC SUGGESTIONS

Use repeatable tests to narrow the cause.

  • Repeat the same scene with the same settings and background tasks.
  • Compare GPU utilisation at different resolutions and at low versus high graphics settings.
  • Monitor individual CPU threads, frame-time consistency, RAM/VRAM pressure, background processes, and power/temperature behaviour.

These are investigation steps, not confirmed findings or upgrade recommendations.

HOW PERFORMANCE LIMITS SHIFT

The same PC can be limited by different things.

Resolution and graphics settings

Higher resolution generally increases GPU rendering work. Lower graphics settings can shift the limiting factor toward CPU-side work. Ray tracing can substantially change GPU demands; results depend on the title and settings.

Frame caps and refresh limits

Frame caps, V-Sync, display refresh limits, and external limiters can reduce utilisation or change frame pacing. Compare uncapped and capped runs carefully, using the same scene and repeatable conditions.

Workloads differ

Games and applications distribute work differently, and the limiting component can change within one workload. A utilisation value is a clue about activity—not a universal bottleneck measurement.

Memory and storage

Paging, memory pressure, and stuttering may be related, but installed capacity or reported VRAM allocation alone does not prove a limitation. Storage can affect loading, streaming, and asset access; faster storage does not necessarily increase average FPS.

Thermals and power

Sustained thermal or power constraints can affect performance. A temperature by itself does not prove throttling: check the exact component limits alongside clock, power, and throttle indicators.

Measurements need context

Capture representative, repeatable measurements over time. Average FPS, 1% lows, utilisation, frame times, settings, and workload context together are more informative than a single snapshot, but still do not guarantee a diagnosis.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

This tool does not calculate bottleneck percentages, compatibility scores, predicted FPS, hardware rankings, or guaranteed upgrade recommendations. It does not measure hardware, inspect running processes, know model-specific thermal limits, or verify power/frequency throttling.

Conditional prompts use heuristic cues only: GPU, RAM, or VRAM utilisation at or above 90%; busiest CPU thread at or above 90%; and GPU utilisation at or below 80% for one possible CPU-side pattern. These are not universal cutoffs or bottleneck percentages.

FPS measurements can vary by scene, run length, capture method, driver, and background activity. A 1% low slightly above average is allowed only within a small tolerance and is flagged for review; recheck the measurement rather than treating it as a performance conclusion.

All analysis runs locally in this browser. Entered system and measurement data are not transmitted or saved by this tool.
