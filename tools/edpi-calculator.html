THE CALCULATION

Effective DPI (eDPI) is the product of your mouse DPI and your in-game sensitivity multiplier.

eDPI = Mouse DPI × In-game sensitivity

Matching eDPI helps maintain consistent aim within the same game, but eDPI varies between games due to different sensitivity scales. Use our Mouse Sensitivity Converter for cross-game comparisons.

eDPI is useful for comparing settings within the same game and sensitivity scale. It is not a universal cross-game standard.
