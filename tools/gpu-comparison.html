READING GPU SPECIFICATIONS

CUDA cores, stream processors, and other execution-unit counts are not directly equivalent. Clock speeds alone do not determine performance; VRAM capacity and bandwidth alone do not determine gaming performance. Ray tracing support does not establish ray tracing speed.

Integrated GPUs may use unified system memory rather than dedicated VRAM. The bandwidth formula below is only used for a dedicated GPU with a selected GDDR6, GDDR6X, GDDR7, or HBM technology and both bus width and effective per-pin data rate supplied.

For that case, theoretical bandwidth (GB/s) = bus width (bits) × effective data rate (Gbps per pin) ÷ 8. The formula assumes a conventional external memory interface and directly applicable effective per-pin rate. Advertised bandwidth is shown separately; rounding, definitions, or architecture can cause differences.

Manufacturer-rated board power is not measured consumption. A recommended PSU is a system-level recommendation, not GPU draw. No performance-per-watt, performance-per-dollar, benchmark, or synthetic score is generated.

All comparisons run locally in this browser. GPU specifications are not fetched, transmitted, or saved.
