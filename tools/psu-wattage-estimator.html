POWER SUPPLY NOTES

Verify more than the wattage label.

Rated wattage and draw

A PSU's rated output capacity is not the same as the power the system continuously draws from the wall. Actual consumption varies with workload and operating conditions.

Efficiency certification

80 PLUS certification describes efficiency under specified test conditions; it does not establish overall electrical quality, reliability, or suitability.

GPU transients

Some GPUs can produce short-duration power spikes that a steady-state sum does not capture. The selected margin cannot guarantee handling of transients.

Connectors and cables

Check required GPU power connectors, CPU EPS connectors, motherboard connectors, cable capacity, and the PSU manufacturer's cabling specifications.

Physical compatibility

Confirm PSU dimensions, mounting, airflow, and cable reach against the case and the rest of the build.

Manufacturer guidance

Consult the GPU manufacturer's PSU recommendation and the PSU manufacturer's specifications for the exact models. This tool does not generate model-specific recommendations.

HOW TO READ THIS ESTIMATE

All component wattages are user-entered estimates, not measured values or product lookups. Ensure the inputs do not double-count power; workload, CPU/GPU boost behavior, storage spin-up, peripherals, and other components can affect actual demand.

The safety margin is a planning allowance only. Confirm continuous output ratings, rail limits, build quality, transient behavior, connectors, GPU maker guidance, and case fit before choosing or reusing a PSU.

All calculations run locally in this browser. Entered component specifications are not transmitted or saved by this tool.
