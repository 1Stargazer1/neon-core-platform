PANEL TECHNOLOGY NOTES

IPS LCD panels are often selected for viewing angles and colour consistency; contrast and response vary by model. VA LCD panels are often associated with higher native contrast, while transition behaviour varies. TN LCD panels can offer fast transitions in some models, with viewing-angle and colour trade-offs.

OLED panels are self-emissive and can produce deep blacks; brightness behavior and image-retention considerations vary. QD-OLED combines OLED emission with quantum-dot colour conversion; results still vary by model. Mini-LED LCD uses a zoned backlight that can support local dimming; zone count and implementation affect results.

Panel technology alone does not establish response time, colour accuracy, contrast, HDR performance, or motion clarity. Actual results vary between individual monitor models.
