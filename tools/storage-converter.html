WHY TWO STANDARDS?

Decimal units use powers of 1,000 and are common on drive packaging and network speeds. Binary units use powers of 1,024; their IEC names are Kibibytes, Mebibytes, Gibibytes, and Tebibytes.

1 KB = 1,000 bytes
